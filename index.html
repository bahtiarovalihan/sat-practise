Smart SAT Tips

  • Eliminate first. Cross out answers you know are wrong before choosing.
  • Plug in answers. On Math questions, test the options in the equation when stuck.
  • Read the question twice. Many errors come from answering a different question.
  • Trust evidence. In Reading, the correct answer is always supported by the passage.
  • Pace yourself. Skip hard questions, mark them, and return if time allows.
  • Review mistakes. Understanding why you missed a question is worth more than new practice.
